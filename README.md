# Robot Benchmark Atlas

`index.html` is a tabbed explainer for twenty-five robot-learning benchmarks: **EgoGym**, **LIBERO**, **LIBERO-Mem**, **LIBERO-Plus**, **LIBERO-PRO**, **LW-RoboCasa-Tasks**, **M3-Bench-robot**, **MemMimic**, **MEMOBench**, **Meta-World**, **MIKASA-Robo**, **MIKASA-Robo-VLA**, **MimicDroid**, **NaVQA**, **RAVEN-QA**, **RLBench**, **RoboCasa**, **RoboCasa GR-1 Tabletop**, **RoboCasa Kitchen**, **RoboCasa365**, **RoboMemArena**, **RoboMME**, **RoboTwin 2.0**, **RoboVQA** and **VLABench**. Six of them are built on RoboCasa: the RoboCasa tab covers the original 2024 release and links to the other five. The landing tab compares them side by side, and its search box (focus it with `/` from anywhere) finds benchmarks by name, card text or anything on their tabs, then opens the chosen one. Each benchmark tab is meant to be understood in about 30 seconds: what kind of benchmark it is, what the data looks like (observation → policy → action), what the tasks are, and how it's scored. Robotics terms have hover or tap definitions for newcomers.

Demo videos live in `media/`. They were re-encoded from each project's public site or repo (robocasa.ai, the MIKASA-Robo README GIFs, libero-project.github.io, the EgoGym README, cap-policy.github.io). The `libero_sim_*.mp4` clips are real simulator frames (agentview + wrist, 256 px, 20 fps) taken from episode 0 of each suite in `lerobot/libero_{spatial,object,goal,10}_image` on Hugging Face. The `robotwin_*.mp4` clips are real demos (head camera, and wrist cameras for the handover) cut from `lerobot/robotwin_unified` on Hugging Face: the first clean episode (index 550·k) and first randomized episode (550·k + 50) of each chosen task. The `rlbench_views.mp4` / `rlbench_tasks.mp4` clips are episode 0 (128 px, 20 fps) of tasks in `hqfang/rlbench-18-tasks`, read straight out of the remote zips with HTTP range requests; `rlbench_task_builder.mp4` and `rlbench_task_grid.jpg` come from the RLBench repo. The `vlabench_*.mp4` clips are real demos from `VLABench/vlabench_primitive_ft_lerobot_video` and `VLABench/vlabench_composite_ft_lerobot_video` (recorded at 10 Hz; composites shown at 2×). The `liberoplus_*.mp4` clips are the OpenVLA-OFT failure rollouts published in the LIBERO-Plus repo (`static/videos/case_study/`), one per perturbation; `liberoplus_grid.mp4` adds an unperturbed LIBERO-Spatial demo as the reference tile. The `metaworld_*.mp4` clips are rendered locally from Meta-World 3.1.1 (MuJoCo, EGL) with its built-in scripted expert policies and LeRobot's `corner2` camera setup (moved camera, image flipped on both axes), the same source as `lerobot/metaworld_mt50`; they play at half speed (40 fps of the 80 Hz control rate) and freeze on success. The `rkitchen_*.mp4` clips are episode 0 (or 1) of the 24 atomic tasks in `nvidia/PhysicalAI-Robotics-GR00T-X-Embodiment-Sim` (`single_panda_gripper.*`, 256 px, 20 fps). The `gr1_*.mp4` clips are episode 0 of each task's head camera in `nvidia/PhysicalAI-Robotics-GR00T-Teleop-Sim`, with the letterbox bars cropped. The `lw_*.mp4` clips are whole episodes cut from `LightwheelAI/Lightwheel-Tasks-G1-WBC` with HTTP range requests, using the episode boundaries in its `meta/episodes` parquet. The `mimicdroid_*.mp4` clips are the simulation rollouts on the MimicDroid project page (`static/videos/sim_evals/`), cropped to the wide view. The `m3bench_*.mp4` clips are cut from the benchmark's own head-camera videos in `ByteDance-Seed/M3-Bench` on Hugging Face (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): the hero is `kitchen_03` 1:05–1:40 in real time with its audio, up to the first question; `m3bench_scenes.mp4` is 3 s from one video of each of the seven room types. M3-Bench-robot is question answering, not control, so its tab shows the answer loop in place of an action vector; the question examples and the `kitchen_03` timeline come from `data/annotations/robot.json` in the M3-Agent repo. `navqa_coda16.mp4` is CODa's own sped-up clip of sequence 16 (one of NaVQA's seven drives) from amrl.cs.utexas.edu/coda, with CODa's 3D box labels burned in; `navqa_remembr_chips.mp4` is cut from the ReMEmbR project page's Nova Carter demo (`static/video/demo.mp4`, 0:36–0:56). The NaVQA question examples and the sequence 16 timeline come from `remembr/data/navqa/data.csv`; the SOTA table is STaR's re-run (arXiv 2602.09255). `raven_hero.mp4` (Go1 frontier exploration, 0:00–0:36, letterbox cropped) and `raven_retrieval.mp4` (questions 1–2 being answered and driven to, 0:00–0:38, HLG tone-mapped to SDR) are cut from the RAVEN project page's `IRoM_exploration_3_3x.mp4` and `IRoM_retrieval_v3_1_3x.mp4`, both already at 3×. `raven_memory_grid.jpg` is all 20 memory frames of `real_world/go1/go1_real_IRoM_v2` from `zzcnewly/RAVEN_QA` on Hugging Face, with the answer frames for each of its 5 questions numbered; `raven_sources.mp4` is 14 memory frames from one video of each source (`habitat_sim_v4/scene1`, `irs/indoor/2JYMWPElMHk`, `irs_hard/indoor/equad_3_part_1`, `go1_real_bowen_hall_full_v1`). RAVEN-QA SOTA numbers are Tables 1, 6 and 8 of the RAVEN paper (arXiv 2606.25206); the scoring rule (2 s for times, 10 cm for positions) is from `raven_qa_eval.py` in the RAVEN repo. The `robomme_*.mp4` clips come from robomme.github.io: `robomme_hero.mp4` is its `video/VideoUnmaskSwap.mp4` (front + wrist camera, red border = the demonstration video); `robomme_tasks.mp4` tiles the front-camera half of all 16 task videos (PatternLock is the page's `DrawPattern.mp4`), each sped up to fit 15 s and frozen on its last frame; `robomme_stopcube.mp4` stacks the page's StopCube episode-1 rollouts of π0.5 and FrameSamp+Modul. The RoboMME SOTA table is the project leaderboard (updated 29 September 2026), with suite columns averaged from its per-task numbers. The `robovqa_*.mp4` clips come from the RoboVQA release in the `gdm-robovqa` Google Cloud bucket (CC BY 4.0): `robovqa_hero.mp4` loops six validation clips, two per embodiment; `robovqa_episode.mp4` plays the five validation clips of the episode "please remove the chips from the bowl and place them in the top draw" in step order, and its Q&A table comes from `json/val`. `robovqa_intervention.mp4` is the project page's `rate_50.0_20a223b06d82cb296928.mp4`. RoboVQA planning numbers are from the arXiv PDF (the project page's table differs slightly); BLEU numbers are as reported by Thinker (arXiv 2601.21199) and RoboBrain (arXiv 2502.21257). The `liberopro_*.mp4` clips tile rollouts from the LIBERO-PRO project page (`static/videos/`): the hero is OpenVLA's LIBERO-Object case study (original, object, position, semantic, task) plus the salad-dressing-replaced-by-soup run; `liberopro_position.mp4` is the x-axis sweep (+0.1 to +0.5); `liberopro_object.mp4` and `liberopro_compose.mp4` are its object and task-composition findings. Short clips hold their last frame. LIBERO-PRO SOTA numbers are the repo leaderboard averaged over the four suites; original-LIBERO scores come from the project page's per-task tables. `libmem_hero.mp4` (T7, agentview | wrist, first 40 s) and `libmem_tasks.mp4` (all 10 tasks, agentview) are cropped from the RGB column of the demo videos on libero-mem.github.io (`static/videos/`). The `memmimic_*.mp4` clips are Gated Memory Policy rollouts from gated-memory-policy.github.io (`assets/videos/`): the hero tiles Match Color, Discrete Place Back, Iterative Pushing (friction 0.005) and Iterative Flinging (2.0 kg); `memmimic_matchcolor.mp4` and `memmimic_casting.mp4` put the no-history Diffusion Policy next to GMP. MemMimic's robot, cameras and 8-number action were checked against `mujoco-env/config` in the GMP repo; its SOTA numbers are read from the project page's result charts. `memobench_overview.jpg` is `assets/teaser.png` from the MEMOBench repo. The `mikasavla_*.mp4` clips are the real 128 px demos in `mikasa-robo/mikasa-robo-vla-lerobot` (top and wrist AV1 streams), cut with the episode lengths in each task's `meta/episodes` parquet; short tasks play whole episodes back to back. `robomemarena_hero.mp4` (the empty-drawer occlusion task, 2×) and `robomemarena_tasks.mp4` (12 tasks, one category per row) are from robomemarena.github.io (`videos/`). LIBERO-Mem SOTA combines the paper's subgoal table with ActMem-VLA (arXiv 2609.37307); RoboMemArena adds RoboFoundry (arXiv 2609.32862). `robocasa_ai_textures.mp4` is from robocasa.ai; `robocasa_turn_on_microwave.mp4` and `robocasa_prepare_coffee.mp4` are re-encoded from `assets/`. Grid clips are sped up to fit and each tile freezes on its last frame.

# Memory worlds (`memory-worlds.html`)

A five-minute visual lesson: **long-horizon is not memory**. Real GIFs and clips, then the action vector `env.step` actually consumes.

This is **not** a code mapping. The call-tree menu lives next door in `menuCodeMapping/` (code_flow, port 8766). That site answers *what our Python did*. This one answers *what the world is*, and whether a policy needs history.

## Open

```bash
bash serve.sh          # http://127.0.0.1:8767/
# or open index.html from disk
```

## What the page teaches

1. **Long ≠ memory.** CALVIN / LIBERO / RoboCasa can run for hundreds of steps with every object still on camera. MIKASA RememberColor hides the cue (the yellow cube).
2. **Three regimes** (MemMimic / Gated Memory Policy): Markovian, in-trial, cross-trial.
3. **Worlds, not papers:** RememberColor, ShellGameTouch, TakeItBack, Intercept (MIKASA-Robo); Match Color, Iterative Pushing, Place Back (MemMimic); CALVIN as the long-but-visible contrast; EgoGym as the 17-D exception.
4. **Action layouts** from published env specs only — no live probe, no invented step values. MIKASA/LIBERO: 7-D `pd_ee_delta_pose`. EgoGym: flattened 4×4 + gripper.

**Gated Memory Policy** and **LaWAM** are policies, not simulators. GMP is evaluated on MemMimic + MIKASA-Robo. LaWAM is evaluated on LIBERO + RoboTwin.

Footage is stored under `assets/` from the projects’ public pages (MIKASA-Robo docs, GMP site, CALVIN repo).

Interactive bits: regime filters (EgoGym is the 17-D action contrast and stays on All), EgoGym card with a live 7-D / 17-D arm switch, dark/light theme. Clone buttons copy the public GitHub URL — those folders are not on this laptop.
